# Infograph template — design notes

Reference: `sketchnote-toolchain.png` ("AI TOOLCHAIN SKETCHNOTES").
This is the approved sketchnote style. Match its energy and density.

## Tokens

- Red `#D92626` · Dark `#1A1A1A` · White background
- Doodle SVGs carry their own pink fills — leave untouched

## Layout

- Big speech-bubble title: rounded rect + open-V tail (two
  segments, no fill — avoids the seam a closed triangle leaves)
- Column layout with central dashed divider; red rounded column
  headers
- Numbered red badges, bold headings, red underline accents,
  2–3 short body lines per block
- Curved arrows (triangle heads) between ideas; one doodle per
  block, placed near the text it illustrates
- Verdict box bottom-center

## Artwork

Real hand-drawn doodles only: Open Doodles (CC0) SVGs from
`~/workspace/tools/sketchnote-doodles/`, placed via VectorCraft
`file.place`. No AI-generated art. Map doodles to blocks by vibe.

## Rules

- Dense but never cluttered; no overlaps, no clipped doodles,
  no orphaned arrows
- Zero image-generation APIs in the pipeline
- Read the PNG and iterate before delivery
